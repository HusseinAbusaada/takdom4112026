Partner: Principles for Peace

Empowering Palestinian Women Leaders for Global Advocacy

Future Pioneers strengthens the leadership and advocacy capacities of Palestinian women leaders from the West Bank.

The program combines practical skill-building with mentorship and networking, helping participants strengthen their ability to advocate for rights and elevate Palestinian women’s voices within their communities and beyond.

Core learning areas

  • Leadership development
  • Public speaking
  • Negotiation
  • Advocacy
  • Strategic mentorship and networking

From skills to influence

The initiative is designed to prepare participants to engage more confidently in decision-making processes and to contribute meaningful change in their communities.
