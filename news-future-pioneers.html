Future Pioneers: Empowering Palestinian Women Leaders for Global Advocacy

News & Stories from Takadom for Peace

Program StoryPalestine / International

In partnership with Principles for Peace, Takadom for Peace implemented the Future Pioneers project to strengthen the capacity of Palestinian women leaders from the West Bank to advocate for their rights.

Participants received training in leadership, public speaking, negotiation and advocacy, supported by mentorship and networking opportunities.

Preparing women to influence change

The initiative strengthened participants’ ability to elevate Palestinian women’s voices within their communities and beyond, helping prepare a new cohort of women leaders for meaningful participation and decision-making.
