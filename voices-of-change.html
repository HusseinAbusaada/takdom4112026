Voices of Change

Empowering Youth and Women for Inclusive Leadership and Dialogue — an activity under Future Pioneers.

Training / Activity • Future Pioneers Partner: European Union and Women of the Sun

Empowering Youth and Women for Inclusive Leadership and Dialogue

Voices of Change strengthens the leadership and civic participation of Palestinian youth and women and creates more inclusive spaces for leadership and dialogue.

Training in Jerusalem

A two-day workshop focused on “Democratic Representation and Active Participation.” Participants took part in interactive activities, group discussions and practical exercises.

Skills strengthened

  • Leadership
  • Dialogue
  • Democratic participation
  • Community engagement
  • Reflection on the role of changemakers
